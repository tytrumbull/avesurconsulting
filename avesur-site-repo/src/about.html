About Avesur Consulting Inc.

Established in 2011, Avesur Consulting Inc. is an Ottawa-based, 100% Canadian-owned and woman-led firm specializing in independent technical validation, cybersecurity assurance, regulatory and trade compliance, and cross-border market access.

25+ Years Senior IT Security Leadership & 40+ Years Team Depth

Since 2011, Avesur has served federal public sector departments, defense organizations, and enterprise clients, bridging the gap between written compliance policies and live production reality. Our technical team brings over 25 years of senior Information Technology (IT) security experience, with the last 6+ years directly focused on hands-on Cloud Architecture (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, XaaS) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) security validation across Government of Canada (GC) enterprise landing zones and multi-cloud networks.

Market Access, Brand & Digital Growth Team

Avesur's market access practice is led by a Canadian former technology journalist with 10+ years building marketing systems that generate leads, and a creative director with 12+ years leading brand and digital design teams. Working in English and Spanish from Mexico's Riviera Maya, the team builds the brands, websites and search presence that carry Canadian, US and Mexican firms into new markets.

How We Assess

We compare what an organization says it does with what its systems, records and people show. We work from the frameworks your buyers and regulators use: ITSG-33 and the Cyber Centre's ITSP.10 series (including the ITSP.10.033 controls catalogue and ITSP.10.171 for CPCSC), the Harmonized TRA Methodology (TRA-1), NIST SP 800-53, 800-171 and 800-30, ISO/IEC 27001, and FedRAMP baselines. We never assess work we built or remediated for the same client.

Corporate Qualifications & Procurement Roadmap

  • Facility Security Clearance (FSC): Active Secret (Level II) Clearance.
  • Ownership: 100% Canadian-owned and woman-led.
  • Federal procurement vehicles: ProServices Supply Arrangement (E60ZT-180024/C) submission under review; SBIPS and the Federal AI Source List (ITQ WS4286933967) on our procurement roadmap.
  • Low-dollar contracting: Eligible federal services engagements up to $40,000 may be contracted non-competitively under section 6(b) of the Government Contracts Regulations, subject to the contracting authority's rules and approvals.
  • Trilingual Operational Delivery: Native technical and executive delivery in English, French, and Spanish (EN / FR / ES).
